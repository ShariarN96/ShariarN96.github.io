Research Focus

From catalyst design to cell-level battery performance.

My Ph.D. work connects materials synthesis, electrochemical testing, and characterization to make ammonia from nitrate pollution and to understand how energy storage materials perform and fail.

MOF catalysts for ammonia

Single-metal and multivariate MOFs reaching ~1 A cm−2 and 90% Faradaic efficiency for nitrate electroreduction.

3D graphene foam electrodes

CVD-grown graphene foam/MOF electrodes delivering 1500 mA cm−2 at neutral pH.

Zinc–nitrate and lithium cells

Cycling over 50+ charge–discharge cycles on Biologic and Gamry potentiostats to track stability and degradation.

Characterization and failure analysis

XPS, SEM/EDS, Raman, XRD, and NMR to find what limits performance in energy materials.
