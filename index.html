University of Illinois Chicago / Chicago, IL / Jun 2021–Present

Electrocatalysts and batteries for nitrate-to-ammonia conversion

Graduate research and teaching assistant designing catalysts, building cells, and diagnosing failure in energy conversion and storage materials.

~1 A cm−2 current density 90% Faradaic efficiency 11 peer-reviewed papers
  • Designed single-metal and multivariate metal–organic framework (MOF) catalysts for nitrate electroreduction, achieving ~1 A cm−2 current density, 90% Faradaic efficiency, and ammonia yield rates of ~68 mg h−1 cm−2 in alkaline media.
  • Synthesized and optimized advanced 3D graphene foam/MOF electrodes using chemical vapor deposition (CVD) for electrochemical energy conversion, achieving 1500 mA cm−2 current density at neutral pH.
  • Constructed and evaluated zinc–nitrate batteries using galvanostatic charge/discharge testing to characterize cycling stability, power density, and degradation behavior across 50+ charge–discharge cycles.
  • Developed electrochemical testing protocols for lithium-based battery systems on Biologic and Gamry potentiostats, using cyclic voltammetry and galvanostatic methods to evaluate reaction kinetics, catalyst stability, degradation behavior, and overall cell performance.
  • Performed materials characterization and electrochemical failure analysis using XPS, SEM/EDS, Raman, XRD, and NMR to identify degradation mechanisms and performance-limiting behavior in energy storage materials.
  • Developed an AI-assisted literature mining platform for MOF-based catalyst discovery, integrating Wiley TDM, CrossRef, Azure Blob Storage, and OpenAI LLMs into a scalable workflow for accelerated materials screening.
  • Published 11 peer-reviewed papers in journals including Science, Nature Communications, Advanced Energy Materials, Advanced Materials, and ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces.

Research Focus

Cleaner chemistry and better batteries, from materials to devices.
